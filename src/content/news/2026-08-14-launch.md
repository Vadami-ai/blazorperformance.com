---
title: "BlazorPerformance.com is live"
date: 2026-08-14
---

The site launches with its three pillars in place: the open-source
[Blazor Server Capacity Calculator](/tools/capacity-calculator/), the first two
playbooks ([capacity planning methodology](/playbooks/capacity-planning-blazor-server/)
and [measuring per-circuit memory](/playbooks/measuring-circuit-memory/)), and a
curated resource directory with 24 annotated references across six problem areas.
The resource directory is itself open source; additions are welcome via pull request.
